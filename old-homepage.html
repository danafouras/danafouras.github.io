Dana Fouras is an artist and composer whose work moves between sound, music, movement and image. Her approach to composition has grown through a long engagement with dance and choreography, and through close creative collaboration across disciplines. Her experience as a performer and long-standing artistic collaborator informs the way she listens, works with rhythm and thinks about space, texture and the relationship between sound and the body. SOUND & COMPOSITION Listening is where it begins. Composition is at the centre of Dana's current creative practice. Her work explores sound, atmosphere, rhythm and texture, informed by an embodied understanding of movement and time. Alongside music created in dialogue with choreography, she is developing an independent body of work and opening new directions for listening, collaboration and commission. CURRENT PRACTICE · AUSTRALIA / UK Developing new connections. Based across Australia and the UK, Dana is developing a new phase of her creative practice, with an interest in exchange, professional artist development and collaborative research. Together with Russell Maliphant Dance Company, she is developing an Australian context for the company's Creative Intensive approach: bringing Australian movement practitioners into direct contact with Russell Maliphant's choreographic practice, while drawing on Dana's long-term knowledge of that practice.
