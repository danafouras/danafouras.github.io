About

Movement, sound
and collaboration.

Dana Fouras is a dancer, composer and creative collaborator working across movement, sound and choreography.

She has a longstanding creative relationship with choreographer Russell Maliphant and is an Artistic Collaborator with Russell Maliphant Dance Company. Her work has developed through performance, dance-making, composition and close collaboration across disciplines.

Practice

Dance

Performance and movement research shaped by an extensive practice in classical and contemporary dance.

Composition

Sound and musical thinking developed in close dialogue with movement, choreography and performance.

Collaboration

Long-term creative relationships connecting dancers, choreographers, sound, image, light and film.

Research

An embodied approach to artistic enquiry, exploring rhythm, space, energy and the relationship between physical and sonic processes.

Australia / UK

Developing new connections.

Based across Australia and the UK, Dana is developing a new phase of her creative practice with a focus on exchange, professional artist development and collaborative movement research.

Together with Russell Maliphant Dance Company, she is developing an Australian context for the company's Creative Intensive approach: bringing Australian movement practitioners into direct contact with Russell Maliphant's choreographic practice, while drawing on Dana's long-term knowledge of that practice.

The intention is to build a sustainable relationship that can grow into future intensives, residencies, research and development, and collaborative creation between Australian and UK artists.
