About

An artistic practice
shaped by movement.

Dana Fouras is an artist and composer whose work moves between sound, music, movement and image. Her approach to composition has grown through a long engagement with dance and choreography, and through close creative collaboration across disciplines.

Through years of performance and award-winning artistic collaboration, she has developed an embodied understanding of rhythm, space, texture and the relationship between sound and the body.

Sound and composition

Composition is at the centre of Dana's current creative practice. Her work explores sound, atmosphere, rhythm and texture, informed by an embodied understanding of movement and time.

Alongside music created in dialogue with choreography, she is developing an independent body of work and opening new directions for listening, collaboration and commission.

Composition · Selected commissions

Composing for performance

Dana's commissioned composition work includes projects with Russell Maliphant Dance Company, National Youth Dance Company, English National Ballet and the Greek National Opera, supported by the Stavros Niarchos Foundation. Her musical practice brings together a distinctive musical sensibility, an instinct for sound and atmosphere, and a deep understanding of the relationship between music and movement.
