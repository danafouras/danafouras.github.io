About

An artistic practice
shaped by movement.

Dana Fouras is an artist and composer whose work moves between sound, music, movement and image. Her approach to composition has grown through a long engagement with dance and choreography, and through close creative collaboration across disciplines.

Through years of performance and award-winning artistic collaboration, she has developed an embodied understanding of rhythm, space, texture and the relationship between sound and the body.

About

The Royal Ballet

Trained in classical dance in Australia and at The Royal Ballet School, Dana Fouras joined The Royal Ballet in 1988, beginning an international performing career across classical and contemporary repertoire. Her subsequent independent career brought long-standing artistic collaborations and extended her work into coaching, rehearsal direction and movement.

During her years with The Royal Ballet, Dana worked with choreographers including Ashley Page, David Bintley and William Forsythe, as well as within the distinctive choreographic world of Sir Kenneth MacMillan.

During her graduate year at The Royal Ballet School, Sir Kenneth MacMillan cast Dana as Lead Girl in Soirée Musicales, a work celebrating the 90th birthday of Dame Ninette de Valois. She went on to perform Juliet in Romeo & Juliet, Mitzi Caspar in Mayerling and the Mistress in Manon. She also worked on MacMillan's productions of The Prince of the Pagodas and Winter Dreams. These experiences gave her a close and lasting connection to his distinctive dramatic and expressive approach to dance.

Her repertoire also included Solo Swan in Swan Lake, and roles in Requiem and My Brother My Sisters, alongside the creation of further roles in works by Ashley Page and David Bintley.

Independent Dance & Collaboration

In 1994, Dana moved into independent dance, working with artists including Irek Mukhamedov, Jonathan Burrows, Wayne McGregor and Russell Maliphant.

Her long-standing collaboration with Russell Maliphant began in 1998 and spanned more than two decades with Russell Maliphant Dance Company. Alongside her work as a dancer, Dana's contribution to the company also encompassed photography, composition, creative collaboration and rehearsal direction.

Coaching & Répétiteur Work

Alongside her performance career, Dana has continued her association with Sir Kenneth MacMillan's repertoire through international coaching and répétiteur work, including engagements with Paris Opera Ballet and Hungarian National Ballet.

Sound and composition

Composition is at the centre of Dana's current creative practice. Her work explores sound, atmosphere, rhythm and texture, informed by an embodied understanding of movement and time.

Composition · Selected commissions

Composing for performance

Dana's commissioned composition work includes projects with Russell Maliphant Dance Company, National Youth Dance Company, English National Ballet and the Greek National Opera, supported by the Stavros Niarchos Foundation. Her compositions draw on an instinct for sound and atmosphere, and a deep understanding of the relationship between music and movement.

Alongside music created in dialogue with choreography, she is developing an independent body of work and opening new directions for listening, collaboration and commission.

The Creative Intensive — Movement, Light & Sound

A collaborative programme by Russell Maliphant and Dana Fouras, exploring movement, light and sound as an integrated creative language.

The programme is open to individual participants and available for international presentation in partnership with dance organisations, festivals, conservatoires and cultural institutions.
